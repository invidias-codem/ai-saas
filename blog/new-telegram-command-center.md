We are excited to announce the launch of our new Telegram command center for Genie! This new feature allows you to control Genie directly from Telegram, making it easier than ever to manage your tasks and get things done.

## Features

*   **Command Execution:** Execute Genie commands directly from Telegram.
*   **Real-time Updates:** Receive real-time updates on the status of your commands.
*   **Easy Integration:** Simple setup process to connect your Genie account to Telegram.

## Getting Started

1.  Add the Genie Telegram bot to your Telegram.
2.  Authenticate with your Genie account.
3.  Start using commands!

## Example Commands

`/genie list tasks` - Lists all your current tasks.
`/genie create task "Buy milk"` - Creates a new task.

We believe that the new Telegram command center will significantly improve your experience with Genie. We are constantly working to improve our platform and provide you with the best possible tools.
