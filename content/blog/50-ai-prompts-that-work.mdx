# 50 AI Prompts That Actually Work (With Real Examples)

<Callout type="info" title="Quick Navigation">
Jump to: [Content Creation](#content-creation-prompts-1-10) | [Coding](#coding-prompts-11-20) | [Analysis](#analysis-prompts-21-30) | [Business](#business-prompts-31-40) | [Creative](#creative-prompts-41-50)

We've all been there—staring at an AI chat window, unsure how to get the response we actually need. The difference between a mediocre AI response and a game-changing one often comes down to **how you ask**.

After months of testing thousands of prompts with Genie AI, ChatGPT, and Claude, we've compiled the **50 most effective prompts** that consistently deliver excellent results. Each prompt is battle-tested, includes real examples, and comes with pro tips to help you get even better outputs.

## How to Use These Prompts

Before diving in, here are three principles that make any prompt more effective:

1. **Be Specific**: Instead of "write about marketing," try "write a 500-word blog post about email marketing for SaaS startups"
2. **Provide Context**: Tell the AI who you are, who your audience is, and what you're trying to achieve
3. **Iterate**: Use follow-up prompts to refine the output—AI conversations are meant to be iterative

<Callout type="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Copy any prompt below and replace the `[BRACKETED]` sections with your specific information. The more detail you provide, the better your results.
</Callout>
