# Supercharging Your Workflow with Lattice OS for Slack

Communication platforms like Slack are the heartbeat of modern teams. But usually, your AI assistant lives in a separate tab, disconnected from where the actual work happens.

**That changes today.**

The Lattice OS for Slack integration has been upgraded. It operates as a context-aware teammate that understands code, remembers conversations, and accesses the team's collective knowledge.

<Callout type="info" title="New Features">
This update brings **Thread-Level Memory**, **Advanced Code Assistance**, and **RAG Integration** directly into your Slack channels.
</Callout>

## 1. Context that Follows the Thread

Most Slack bots are "amnesic"—they forget what you said one message ago. Lattice OS now implements **Thread-Level Memory**.

When you reply to Lattice OS in a thread, it automatically reads the full conversation history. You can ask follow-up questions like "Can you optimize that function?" or "What did you mean by the second point?" without restating context.

**Try it:**
> @Lattice OS "List three marketing ideas for Q1."
> *Lattice OS responds...*
> "Expand on the first one."

## 2. Your New AI Pair Programmer

Lattice OS for Slack now uses our specialized **Code Assistant** model. It can detect when you're asking about code and switches modes to provide more precise, syntactically correct, and secure implementations.

You can ask Lattice OS to:
- **Debug errors**: Paste a stack trace and ask for a fix.
- **Explain legacy code**: Ask "What does this snippet do?"
- **Refactor**: Request cleanups for readability or performance.

Lattice OS intelligently formats code blocks with syntax highlighting, making them easy to read and copy directly from Slack.

## 3. Tap into Your Team's Brain (RAG)

The most power feature in this update is the **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** integration.

When you ask a question, Lattice OS doesn't just rely on its training data. It searches your team's connected knowledge base—past documents, successful code snippets, and saved memories—to give an answer that is specific to *your* company.

> **User**: "@Lattice OS How do we handle authentication in our API?"
> **Lattice OS**: "Based on your `auth-service` docs, you use JWTs with a 15-minute expiration. Here is the standard middleware pattern..."

## Getting Started

To enable these features, simply update your Slack app integration from the Lattice OS Dashboard.

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Click **Update Slack App**.
3. Authorize the new scopes (required for reading thread history).

We can't wait to see what you build with Lattice OS in Slack!
