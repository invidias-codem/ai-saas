# Introducing Universal Import: Bring Your ChatGPT and Gemini Memories Home to Lattice OS

### Stop fragmenting your thoughts across different AI platforms. Consolidate your history and jumpstart Lattice OS’s memory of you in minutes.

Let’s face it: you probably cheat on Lattice OS.

You have long coding threads in ChatGPT, brainstorming sessions in Claude, and deep research dives in Google Gemini. We get it—different models have different strengths.

But this creates a problem: **Context fragmentation.**

When you switch platforms, you start from zero. You have to re-explain your project goals, your coding style preferences, and the backstory of what you’re working on. Your valuable conversational history is trapped in silos.

## Today, we are tearing down those silos.

Lattice OS Universal Import is now available. It allows operators to take ownership of their data and consolidate conversational history into one unified Memory Bank.

### It’s More Than Just Storage. It’s Memory.

When you import your history from other platforms, Lattice OS doesn't just shove the text into a dusty archive.

Our import engine **actively processes your old conversations** to kickstart Lattice OS’s understanding of you. During the import process, Lattice OS will:

1.  **Index your history** for semantic search (so you can find that one idea from six months ago).
2.  **Extract key facts** about your projects and goals.
3.  **Learn your preferences** and communication style.

By importing your history, you are effectively giving Lattice OS months (or years) of context about who you are in a matter of minutes.

## Here is how to get started.

### Part 1: Exporting Your Data

First, you need to liberate your data from the other platforms. It’s easier than you think.

#### 🟢 How to export from OpenAI (ChatGPT)

OpenAI makes it relatively simple to get a copy of every conversation you've ever had with them.

1.  Log in to ChatGPT and click on your profile icon in the bottom left.
2.  Go to **Settings & Beta > Data controls**.
3.  Click the **Export data** button.
4.  Confirm the request. OpenAI will email you a link when your download is ready (this can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours depending on your history size).
5.  Download the `.zip` file from the email.

*(Note: You don't even need to unzip it! Lattice OS's smart importer can handle the whole archive.)*

![Screenshot of ChatGPT Data Controls settings page indicating the Export button](/blog/chatgpt-export-settings.png)

#### 🔵 How to export from Google Gemini

Google uses its standard "Takeout" service for Gemini data.

1.  Head over to [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com/).
2.  At the top of the list, click **Deselect all** (otherwise you'll download your entire Google life!).
3.  Scroll down alphabetically until you find **Gemini Apps** and check the box next to it.
4.  Scroll to the very bottom and click **Next step**.
5.  Choose "Send download link via email" and click **Create export**.
6.  Wait for the email and download your `.zip` file.

![Screenshot of Google Takeout interface with Gemini Apps selected](/blog/google-takeout-gemini.png)

### Part 2: Importing into Lattice OS

Now for the easy part. We’ve built a new "Smart Drop Zone" that handles the heavy lifting for you.

1.  Log in to Lattice OS and navigate to **Settings > Data & Memory**.
2.  Look for the new **Import History** section.
3.  Simply drag the `.zip` file you downloaded from ChatGPT or Gemini directly onto the drop zone.

Lattice OS’s smart detection will automatically analyze the file structure to figure out which platform it came from.

![Screenshot of the new Lattice OS Import Wizard showing the "Smart Drop Zone" with a file hovering over it](/blog/genie-import-wizard.png)

Review the summary and click **Start Import**.

Watch as Lattice OS processes your history. You'll see real-time metrics showing you exactly what's happening:

> ✅ **Processing:** 1,420 Conversations found...
>
> ✅ **Parsing:** 25,300 Messages processed...
>
> 🧠 **Learning:** 185 New Facts & Memories extracted!

Once complete, your historical data is instantly searchable within Lattice OS, and your Memory Bank will be richer with new context about you.
