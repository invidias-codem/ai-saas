*By Joshua Jair*

---

The old positioning was about infrastructure sovereignty. That framing was accurate, but it described the chassis instead of the vehicle. What we actually ship is not a self-hosted appliance — it is a **curated expert workspace**: a domain-specific execution environment with a pre-loaded knowledge substrate, quarantined mutations, and a visible reasoning trace.

Today we are migrating the brand to match the product.

---

## The Migration

The root landing page is now the **Expert as a Service** experience. The sovereign infrastructure story is not gone; it has been reframed as the execution layer underneath the expert. That is where it belongs. Operators hire expertise because of outcomes, not infrastructure.

The new landing page follows a simple flow:

1. **Hook** — Rentable intellect. Precision execution.
2. **The Roster** — Specialized operators for specific domains: production, infrastructure, commerce.
3. **The Moat** — Sandboxed execution, refined data forgery, trajectory transparency, precision tool use.
4. **CTA** — Initialize a session. Three steps. No sales call.

Every section is translated and componentized. The Roster Grid is reusable; the same component can surface on the onboarding page or in future marketplace views without rewriting layout logic.

---

## What Changed in the Stack

- **`components/marketing/RosterGrid.tsx`** — discrete expert card grid with role, title, description, and tags
- **`app/[locale]/(landing)/expert/page.tsx`** — the new preview landing page, currently mounted at `/{locale}/expert`
- **`messages/en.json`** — all new copy externalized under `Landing.expert.*` for i18n scaling

The old Lattice OS landing page remains intact at `app/[locale]/(landing)/page.tsx` until the final promotion review is complete.

---

## The Moat, Restated

The technical capabilities that differentiate this platform from a generic LLM wrapper are now positioned as premium features, not backend documentation:

- **Sandboxed Execution** — mutations are quarantined and integrity-verified before promotion
- **Refined Data Forgery** — dynamic RAG isolates context boundaries so the expert reasons strictly from curated sources
- **Trajectory Transparency** — observe the raw reasoning stream and inspect the agent trajectory before accepting the output
- **Precision Execution** — clinical tool use, no approximation, rentable intellect for operators who need exact outputs

These are not marketing abstractions. They are live behaviors in the stack: `LocalSandboxRunner`, `QuarantinePromotionManager`, `useConversationStream`, and the SSE stream contract in `app/api/conversation/route.ts`.
